# Research

Our research line addresses the development and validation of emerging X-ray imaging technologies for medical applications. We work across five interconnected areas, ranging from instrumentation development to computational validation through *virtual clinical trials*. Our methodology combines hardware, Monte Carlo simulation, deep learning, and preclinical experimentation, with a strong translational component in collaboration with hospitals and clinical partners.

---

### [XTALIS]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/xtalis)

Grating-based phase contrast X-ray imaging system. Our home-built setup based on Talbot–Lau interferometry, applied to breast and chest imaging. [Read more &rarr;]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/xtalis)

### [Monte Carlo simulation]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/simulation)

Geant4 and GAMOS pipelines for medical image simulation, including the **MIMAC** code for digital mammography integrated with the VICTRE phantom. [Read more &rarr;]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/simulation)

### [Virtual Clinical Trials]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/vct)

End-to-end framework for *in silico* evaluation of imaging technologies, integrating digital phantoms, simulation engines, and virtual observers. [Read more &rarr;]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/vct)

### [Deep Learning]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/deep-learning)

Synthetic data generation, image processing, and reconstruction tasks based on neural networks applied to medical imaging. [Read more &rarr;]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/deep-learning)

### [Preclinical imaging]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/preclinical)

Experimental validation of new imaging modalities in preclinical models, with focus on breast and chest pathologies. [Read more &rarr;]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/preclinical)
