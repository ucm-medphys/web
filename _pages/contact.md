## Joining the group

We welcome enquiries from prospective **PhD students**, **postdocs**, and **master students** with a background in physics, biomedical engineering, medical physics, or related fields, and an interest in medical imaging, Monte Carlo simulation, or deep learning applied to radiology.

Please send an email to [garcia.pinto@ucm.es](mailto:garcia.pinto@ucm.es) with a brief description of your background and interests, and attach your CV. For PhD and postdoc positions, mention in the subject line whether you are applying for a specific advertised position or making an open enquiry.

See the [Group]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/team/) page for current members, and the [Openings]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/openings/) page for current advertised positions.
