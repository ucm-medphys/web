# Preclinical imaging

**Experimental validation in breast and chest models.**

The development of a new imaging modality requires experimental validation in realistic biological models. Our preclinical work bridges the gap between computational methods and clinical translation, providing the *ex vivo* and preclinical studies that ground our methodological developments in real tissue and establish the diagnostic added value of new modalities over current clinical practice.

---

## Why preclinical validation?

Monte Carlo simulation can accurately model photon transport and detector response, but biological tissue is complex, heterogeneous, and variable in ways that are difficult to capture computationally. *Ex vivo* and preclinical studies with real tissue specimens provide the ground truth that simulation alone cannot replace, allowing us to:

- Verify that the contrast advantages predicted by simulation are realised in practice
- Identify limitations or artefacts specific to real tissue that are not captured by phantoms
- Develop and validate image quality metrics directly on biologically relevant targets
- Build a translational evidence base for clinical adoption of phase contrast imaging

---

## What we do

- **Ex vivo studies with XTALIS.** Acquisition and analysis of phase contrast images of *ex vivo* breast and chest specimens using our [XTALIS]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/xtalis/) system. This includes fresh and fixed specimens with a range of pathologies (tumours, microcalcifications, fibrous tissue, emphysematous lung).
- **Image quality assessment across contrast channels.** Quantitative comparison of the diagnostic information provided by absorption, differential phase, and dark-field channels, using both physical metrics (CNR, SNR, spatial resolution) and task-based approaches (observer studies).
- **Comparison with simulation.** Cross-validation of preclinical image features against Monte Carlo predictions, contributing to the iterative refinement of our [simulation pipelines]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/simulation/).
- **Translational collaboration.** Joint work with the [Hospital Universitario 12 de Octubre](https://www.imas12.es/) (specimen procurement, clinical guidance, pathology correlation) and other clinical and academic partners in Madrid, with the goal of identifying use cases where new modalities offer clear diagnostic added value.

---

## Breast cancer imaging project (2025)

Our current project, *Preclinical Experiments for Development and Optimization of New Imaging Modalities for Breast Cancer*, focuses on the characterisation of breast tissue specimens under the three contrast channels available in phase contrast imaging, with particular attention to the detection of microcalcifications and the differentiation of fibroglandular tissue types — two tasks of direct clinical relevance in screening mammography.
