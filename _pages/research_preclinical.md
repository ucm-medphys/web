# Preclinical imaging

**Experimental validation in breast and chest models.**

The development of a new imaging modality is incomplete without experimental validation in realistic biological models. Our preclinical work bridges the gap between simulation and clinical translation, providing the *ex vivo* and preclinical studies that ground our methodological developments in real tissue.

## What we do

- **Ex vivo studies.** Acquisition and analysis of phase contrast images of *ex vivo* breast and chest specimens with our XTALIS system.
- **Image quality assessment.** Comparison of the diagnostic information provided by absorption, differential phase, and dark-field channels in conditions relevant to mammography and lung imaging.
- **Translational collaboration.** Joint work with the [Hospital Universitario 12 de Octubre](https://www.imas12.es/) and other clinical and academic partners in Madrid, with the goal of identifying use cases where new modalities offer clear added value over current clinical practice.
