# Group Members

We are based at the [Faculty of Medicine](https://medicina.ucm.es/), Universidad Complutense de Madrid, within the [Medical Physics &amp; Biomedical Instrumentation Research Group](https://medicina.ucm.es/grupos/grupo/446) (Group 920560).

**We welcome PhD students, postdocs, and master students interested in our research.** See our [Contact]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/contact/) page for more information.
