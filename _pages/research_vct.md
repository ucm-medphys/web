# Virtual Clinical Trials

**In silico evaluation of imaging technologies.**

Validating a new imaging modality clinically is slow and expensive. *Virtual clinical trials* (VCT) provide a complementary path: a computational framework that integrates **digital patient models**, **physically accurate simulation**, and **virtual observers**, allowing us to compare imaging technologies, optimize acquisition parameters, and explore clinical scenarios *in silico* before any patient exposure.

## What we do

- **Framework development.** We are building an end-to-end VCT pipeline that integrates digital phantoms, our Monte Carlo image simulation chain, and observer models for task-based image quality assessment.
- **Integration with existing platforms.** We use and contribute to established VCT resources such as the **VICTRE** project (FDA), in particular the breast phantom, and combine them with our own simulation pipelines.
- **Applications.** Comparison of imaging modalities (e.g. digital mammography vs. tomosynthesis vs. phase contrast), optimization of acquisition geometries, and evaluation of AI-based reconstruction or detection methods.
- **Synthetic cohort generation.** Production of virtual patient populations as controlled inputs for AI development and benchmarking.
