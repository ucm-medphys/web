# XTALIS

**Grating-based phase contrast X-ray imaging.**

XTALIS is our home-built X-ray phase contrast imaging system, based on Talbot–Lau grating interferometry with a micro-focus laboratory X-ray source. The system has been developed in-house and is currently used as a research platform for new imaging modalities applied to breast and chest pathologies.

## Why phase contrast?

Conventional radiography exploits the absorption of X-rays in tissue, which provides limited contrast between soft tissues. **Phase contrast imaging** complements absorption by exploiting the refractive index of materials, producing additional contrast channels —differential phase contrast and dark-field— that reveal information not accessible to absorption alone. This is particularly valuable in mammography and lung imaging, where subtle tissue differences are diagnostically relevant.
