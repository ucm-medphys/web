# Deep Learning

**Neural networks applied to medical imaging.**

Deep learning is a transversal tool in our research, used both as a research object —to develop new methods specific to the challenges of medical X-ray imaging— and as a technical instrument across the rest of our research areas.

## What we do

- **Synthetic data generation.** Combining our Monte Carlo simulation pipelines with generative models to produce realistic, labeled synthetic datasets that can be used to train and benchmark image-analysis methods, addressing the scarcity of annotated medical image data.
- **Image processing and reconstruction.** Methods for noise reduction, super-resolution, and image reconstruction tasks specific to phase contrast and mammographic imaging.
- **Integration with virtual clinical trials.** Use of deep-learning models as components of our [VCT pipeline]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research/vct) — both as virtual observers and as image-processing modules under evaluation.
- **AI-aware imaging system design.** Joint optimization of imaging parameters and the AI methods that consume the resulting images.
