# Teaching

The members of the UCM X-Ray Imaging Group contribute to teaching at the **Facultad de Medicina** of the Universidad Complutense de Madrid, primarily in subjects related to medical physics, biomedical instrumentation, and radiation physics.

---

## Student projects (TFM / TFG)

We regularly supervise **Master's theses (TFM)** and **Bachelor's theses (TFG)** in topics related to our research areas:

- X-ray phase contrast imaging and grating interferometry
- Monte Carlo simulation for medical imaging (Geant4 / GAMOS)
- Virtual clinical trials and in silico evaluation
- Deep learning applied to medical image processing
- Preclinical image quality assessment

If you are a student at UCM or another institution interested in any of these topics, please get in touch via the [Contact]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/contact/) page.

---

## PhD training

We train doctoral researchers in the framework of the UCM PhD programmes. PhD projects in our group combine experimental work (the XTALIS system), computational methods (Monte Carlo simulation, deep learning), and translational research in collaboration with our clinical partners.

For enquiries about PhD positions, see the [Openings]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/openings/) page.
